01 · Strategic Diagnosis

Understanding the adoption gap: three customer behavior hypotheses to investigate.

Rather than assuming internal data, here are the behavioral friction points I would audit to understand why shipped features remain underutilized.

Hypothesis 1

The Volunteer Attention Constraint

Management Committee members are volunteers with demanding day jobs. They typically review society affairs in limited windows outside work hours. Technical changelogs that read like software specifications are quickly bypassed unless immediate volunteer relief is obvious.

Investigation Focus: Audit engagement rates of release announcements across committee communication channels.
Hypothesis 2

The Core Module Gravitational Pull

Communities actively rely on core essentials like gate approvals and maintenance payments, but advanced ERP capabilities (such as automated CAM reconciliation or multilingual complaint routing) often experience an adoption drop-off without proactive post-launch enablement.

Investigation Focus: Map the user journey from basic gate usage to deeper Association ERP workflows.
Hypothesis 3

The Collateral Refresh Velocity

When engineering releases updates continuously, sales enablement collateral (battlecards, pitch decks, USP sheets) can easily fall out of sync, leaving commercial teams without verified proof points during high-stakes competitive renewals or tenders.

Investigation Focus: Establish a direct SLA between sprint releases and sales collateral updates.
